Koto is a privacy-first task manager built for Nextcloud. No ads, no tracking, no cloud lock-in — just your to-dos, synced the way CalDAV was meant to work.

YOUR TASKS, YOUR SERVER
Connect directly to your Nextcloud instance over CalDAV. Koto syncs your tasks in the background and keeps a local cache, so your lists are always available, even offline.

BUILT FOR HOW YOU ACTUALLY WORK
An Agenda screen shows a dated timeline across every list. A Tasks screen groups everything into Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This Week, Later and No Date, with Today, Upcoming, All, Done and Everything filters — counts always visible. A Lists screen shows all your task lists with cover images, icons and task totals.

RICH TASK DETAILS
Set due and start dates, priority, repeat rules, progress, location, notes, tags, subtasks, and cross-app links — all stored as standard VTODO so other CalDAV clients see the same data. Recurring tasks roll forward on completion instead of splitting the series.

REMINDERS THAT WORK
Local notifications with configurable offsets — on time, 1 hour, 1 day or 1 week before. Reminders survive reboots and stay in sync when you complete or delete tasks on any device. A Complete action on the notification ticks the task off without opening the app.

CONNECTED TO THE REST OF YOUR SUITE
Link a task directly to a Vinci contact, a Nóta note or a Tavla card. Send a task's title and description to Nóta or Tavla in one tap. Zaman (Nextcloud Calendar) can show Koto due-dates in its own agenda.

MAKE IT YOURS
Smart quick add reads "tomorrow", "friday 17:30", "!2" and "#tag" out of what you type. Manual ordering lets you drag tasks into place; the order syncs to Nextcloud Tasks too. Tag management lets you rename or recolor a tag across every task at once.

NO COMPROMISES ON PRIVACY
Koto talks only to your Nextcloud server. Nothing is sent anywhere else — no accounts to create, no analytics running in the background. It is free, open-source, and built for people who want to own their own data.

FEATURES
- Direct CalDAV sync with your own Nextcloud, no DAVx5/ICSx bridge required
- Agenda, Tasks and Lists screens
- Today, Upcoming, All, Done and Everything filters with live counts
- Rich task details: due, start, priority, repeat, progress, location, notes, tags, subtasks
- Local reminders with configurable offsets, restored after reboot
- Smart quick add with natural-language date and tag parsing
- Tag management: rename, recolor or remove a tag across all tasks at once
- Location reminders on arrival, using AOSP LocationManager (no Google Play Services)
- Attachments uploaded to your own Nextcloud
- Linked items: Vinci contacts, Nóta notes, Tavla cards, or any URL
- Send to Nóta / Send to Tavla cross-posting
- Manual task ordering, synced as X-APPLE-SORT-ORDER
- Offline-first with conflict-aware sync
- Portable: settings and covers restore automatically on a new device
- Biometric lock
- Light / Dark / OLED themes, adjustable text size
- Local-only mode with export/import backup
